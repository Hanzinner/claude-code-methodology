# Decision NNNN · YYYY-MM-DD · short title

**Status:** current | replaced by #N | deferred
**Who:** the person or role that decided

What was decided, in one or two lines.

**Why:** the reason, in one or two lines. What it replaces or rules out, if anything.

**Where it lives:** the file, mechanism or todo this decision points to (optional).

> ADR format: one file is one decision; the number is never reused; a replaced decision is not deleted, its status becomes "replaced by #N". A decision not in the journal does not exist.
